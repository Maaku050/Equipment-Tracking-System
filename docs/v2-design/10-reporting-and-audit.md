# 10. Reporting, audit, search, and pagination

## Reporting classes

| Class | Purpose | Freshness | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operational | Act now on current state | Near-current; target TBD | Pending approvals, pickup queue, due/overdue, returns, maintenance holds, stock exceptions |
| Historical/management | Trends and stewardship | Defined reporting cutoff | Utilization, loan volume, turnaround, damage/loss, maintenance, verified payments |
| Audit/compliance | Explain who did what and why | Immutable, append-oriented | Role changes, decisions, transfers, adjustments, destructive operations |

## Scope requirements

- Borrower: own requests, loans, returns, notices, and permitted accountability records.
- Laboratory: owned/operated inventory and workflows.
- Department/college: approved roll-up of child scopes, honoring sensitive-data limits.
- Campus: explicitly privileged aggregate or record-level view.
- Reports retain historical organization context; a later reorganization must not rewrite old facts.

## MVP report candidates

Inventory/availability by owner/location/state; pending/pickup/open/overdue loans; borrower history; damage/loss; maintenance holds; stock reconciliation; charge assessments and verified payments if enabled; role assignments; and audit action search. Advanced utilization and forecasting are future.

## Audit event requirements

Audit events are generated by the backend and cannot be modified by ordinary application roles. Each material event records:

- immutable event ID and timestamp;
- authenticated actor or service identity;
- actor's effective role/scope where useful;
- entity type/ID and organization scope;
- action/event type and outcome;
- correlation/request ID and idempotency key reference;
- policy/version or reason/reference where relevant;
- safe change payload (selected before/after values or event facts);
- source IP/user agent only under approved privacy/retention policy.

Never log passwords, session tokens, raw credentials, full payment data, or unnecessary message bodies. Reads of particularly sensitive reports may themselves require access audit.

Minimum audited events: request creation/decision/cancellation; allocation/checkout/return/extension; damage/loss; equipment create/edit/transfer/retire/delete; inventory adjustment/reconciliation; account status, membership and role changes; charge assessment/adjustment/waiver/payment; policy changes; exports; and administrative corrections.

## Search, filtering and pagination

All list/report endpoints must apply authorization scope in the database query before pagination. Likely query dimensions:

| Entity | Filters/sorts likely required |
|---|---|
| Equipment | scope, owner, location, type/category, state, condition, availability, tag/serial, updated/name |
| Requests | requester, owner/operating scope, status, date range, approver queue, pickup expiry |
| Loans | borrower, scope, open/overdue/closed, due/checkout/return dates, equipment |
| Maintenance | scope, state, priority, unit/type, assignee, due/open dates |
| Charges/payments | borrower, scope, status, reason, date, source loan |
| Audit | scope, actor, action, entity, date, correlation ID |

Use stable server-side sorting and cursor/keyset pagination for operational feeds; offset pagination may be acceptable for bounded reports. Search semantics, page maximums, export limits, and exact PostgreSQL indexes are Phase 2 decisions informed by expected volume and query plans. No browser should download a campus collection to filter locally.

## Export and privacy

Exports are server-generated, authorized, size-limited, time-limited for download, and audited. Columns and PII depend on role/scope. Long-running exports may be asynchronous. Report definitions must state whether amounts are assessments or verified payments.
